# Generic research query example

**Coach input:**

`طفل عنده توحد ويصرخ عندما يقترب الماء من وجهه وأريد تعليمه النفخ في المياه. ما أفضل الأساليب والتدريبات؟`

**Expected system interpretation:**

- Population: child / autism-related adaptive swimming context.
- Observed difficulty: distress when water approaches face.
- Target skill: blowing bubbles / controlled exhalation in water.
- Research domains: adaptive swimming, autism and water safety, behavioral teaching strategies, modeling/prompting/visual structure, breathing skill acquisition, motor learning.
- No child name or identifying information required.

**Expected result structure:**

1. Safety gate.
2. What the evidence says.
3. Practical progression options.
4. Coach cues.
5. Signs to progress, simplify or stop.
6. Evidence source and date for each evidence-backed point.
7. Limitations and uncertainty.
8. Option to save the result as a private coaching note.

The result is coaching support, not diagnosis or medical treatment.

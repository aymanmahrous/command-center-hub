# Coach Brain private-data boundary

## Generic research

No child name, phone, email, exact address, or other direct identifier is needed. Queries describe the learning problem and goal only.

## Private swimmer case

A named case may be stored only in the authenticated staff workspace according to existing authorization and RLS rules. Private observations and notes are operational records, not public content.

## Public content

Public SEO articles may cover general educational subjects such as adaptive swimming and water safety. They must be generated from reviewed general knowledge and must not contain private swimmer case details.

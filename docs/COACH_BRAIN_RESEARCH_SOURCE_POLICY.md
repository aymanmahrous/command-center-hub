# Coach Brain evidence source policy

Use a source hierarchy for research-backed coaching guidance:

1. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses.
2. Controlled trials and well-designed primary studies.
3. Professional clinical or water-safety guidance from recognized organizations.
4. High-quality educational resources for implementation context.

For each recommendation, distinguish **evidence** from **coach interpretation**. Never state that a study proves a specific child will respond to a drill.

For autism, disability and water-safety topics, explicitly track generalization/maintenance and study limitations when reported. For aquatic rehabilitation, professional scope and referral boundaries take precedence over generic coaching advice.

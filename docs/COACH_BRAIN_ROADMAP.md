# Coach Brain roadmap

## Phase 1 — Safety foundation

Existing feature branch: case form, TRAIN/ADAPT/REFER/STOP gate, non-diagnostic boundary, no forced submersion.

## Phase 2 — Research + knowledge

This branch adds the contract for anonymized research briefs, evidence provenance, source hierarchy, knowledge domains and private/public data boundaries.

## Phase 3 — Product integration

Connect the research brief to an approved research provider/edge function, store reviewed evidence records, add generic-search mode to the UI, and enforce staff authorization for private case records.

## Phase 4 — Longitudinal coaching

Add swimmer skill history, session notes, progress criteria, saved drills and next-session planning. Keep private records behind RLS and role permissions.

## Phase 5 — Research maintenance

On-demand or scheduled evidence refresh with human review for sensitive guidance. No polling and no automatic outbound publishing.
